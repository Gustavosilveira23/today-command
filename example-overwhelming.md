## Research Digest

### Continuous Discovery
- **[Stop Asking About the Past](https://www.producttalk.org/)** (Teresa Torres) - Teresa argues that questions about past behavior are less reliable than direct observation. Summary: focus on what people do, not on what they say they do. 4 practical examples of how to reframe interview questions.
- **[The Discovery/Delivery Continuum](https://www.producttalk.org/)** (Teresa Torres) - new framework for balancing discovery and delivery within the same sprint. Useful for small teams that can't separate the two.

### Behavioral Design
- **[Friction as a Feature](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dbenoni)** (Dan Benoni) - when to add friction instead of removing it. Applications in onboarding and checkout.
- **[The Power of Defaults](https://www.linkedin.com/in/louisxavierl/)** (Louis-Xavier Lavallee) - case study of a fintech that increased conversion by 23% just by changing defaults.

### Design & UX
- **[Empty States Best Practices 2026](https://www.nngroup.com/)** (NN/g) - complete guide with 12 tested patterns. Includes quantitative research from 200 sites.
- **[Microinteractions Revisited](https://www.nngroup.com/)** (NN/g) - 15min article on the state of the art in microinteractions.

### Tech Trends
